MOT info

MOT questions

How do I book?

Ring us on 01484 546693, Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:30pm. If you can't talk now, leave your number and we'll ring you back.

Can I get my MOT done early?

Yes. You can have it done up to a month (minus a day) before your current MOT runs out and keep the same renewal date.

What if my car fails?

We'll tell you what failed and can do the repairs here. If the car stays with us for the repairs and is retested within 10 working days, the partial retest is free.

Which vehicles can you test?

Class 4: cars, vans and motorhomes with up to 12 passenger seats, and goods vehicles up to 3,000kg.
