FAQs

Good to know

The questions we get asked most. Anything else, just give us a ring.

How do I book?

Ring us on 01484 546693, Monday to Friday between 8:00am and 4:30pm, and we'll find you a day. If you can't talk right now, leave your number and we'll ring you back.

Can I get my MOT done early?

Yes. You can get an MOT up to a month (minus a day) before your current one runs out and keep the same renewal date. Not sure when yours is due? Check it here.

What happens if my car fails its MOT?

We'll explain what failed and what it'll take to put right, and we can do the repairs here. Under GOV.UK rules, if you leave the car with us for the repairs and it's retested within 10 working days, the partial retest is free.

Which vehicles can you MOT?

We're a Class 4 test centre. That covers cars, vans and motorhomes with up to 12 passenger seats, and goods vehicles up to 3,000kg.

I've got a warning light on. Can you look at it?

Yes. We'll plug in the diagnostic kit, find out what's behind it and tell you what needs doing before we go ahead.

Are you open at weekends?

No, we're open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:30pm, and closed on Saturdays and Sundays.
